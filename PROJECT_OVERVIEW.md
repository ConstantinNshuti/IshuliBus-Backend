
# iShuliBus – Project Overview

## 1. Project Information

Project Name: iShuliBus

Project Type:
School Transportation Management Platform

Initial Country:
Rwanda

Long-Term Region:
East Africa

Target Platforms:
Android, iOS, and future web-based administration.

Project Status:
Early development.

## 2. Vision

To make school transportation safer, more transparent,
and easier to manage for schools, parents, drivers,
and students across East Africa.

iShuliBus aims to connect schools, transportation
operators, and families through one digital platform.

## 3. Problem Statement

School transportation can present several challenges:

- Parents may not know where the school bus is.
- Parents may not know whether a child boarded safely.
- Schools may lack centralized transport management.
- Drivers may rely on paper-based student lists.
- Transportation delays may be difficult to communicate.
- Schools may lack reliable transportation reports.
- Emergency communication may be inefficient.

These are product hypotheses that should be
validated with schools and parents in Rwanda.

## 4. Proposed Solution

iShuliBus will provide a centralized platform
for managing and monitoring school transportation.

Core capabilities:

1. School administration
2. Student transportation assignments
3. Driver and vehicle management
4. Route management
5. Trip management
6. Live bus tracking
7. Boarding and drop-off confirmation
8. Parent notifications
9. Transport reporting

## 5. Target Users

### 5.1 Schools

Schools manage transportation operations.

Responsibilities:

- Register students
- Manage transportation assignments
- Manage buses
- Manage drivers
- Configure routes
- Monitor active trips
- Access reports

### 5.2 Parents and Guardians

Parents monitor transportation associated
with their authorized children.

Capabilities:

- View assigned school bus
- Follow an active trip
- Receive delay notifications
- Receive boarding notifications
- Receive drop-off notifications
- View relevant transport information

### 5.3 Drivers

Drivers operate assigned school transportation routes.

Capabilities:

- View assigned vehicle
- View assigned route
- Start a trip
- Update trip status
- Record boarding events
- Record drop-off events
- End a trip

### 5.4 Students

Students are transportation participants.

Initial MVP:
Students do not need individual accounts.

Future functionality:
QR-code-based boarding identification.

### 5.5 Platform Administrators

Platform administrators manage:

- School onboarding
- Platform configuration
- Subscription administration
- Support operations
- Platform monitoring

Administrative access must be limited
and auditable.

## 6. Geographic Expansion

### Phase 1 – Rwanda

Objectives:

- Validate the business model
- Pilot with selected schools
- Gather parent and driver feedback
- Validate GPS tracking reliability
- Validate boarding workflows
- Establish operational support

### Phase 2 – Regional Expansion

Potential markets:

- Uganda
- Kenya
- Tanzania

Country-specific requirements must be evaluated
before launch.

### Phase 3 – East African Scale

Expand the platform to additional markets
based on commercial demand, regulatory requirements,
and operational readiness.

## 7. Business Model

Primary model:

Monthly subscription per active student.

Example:

Monthly Revenue =
Number of Billable Active Students
× Monthly Price per Student

Pricing has not yet been finalized.

Important business decisions still required:

- Definition of a billable active student
- Monthly pricing
- School billing cycle
- Free trial policy
- Payment methods
- Cancellation policy
- Refund policy
- Tax handling
- School contracts

The initial MVP may use manual invoicing
before introducing online payment integration.

## 8. Supported Languages

Initial languages:

- English
- Kinyarwanda

The mobile application should be designed
for localization from the beginning.

Avoid hardcoded user-facing text.

Future language support may include
other regional languages.

## 9. Key Product Principles

### Safety

Protect children and their location information.

### Privacy

Only authorized users may access student
and transportation information.

### Reliability

Transportation status must remain understandable
even when mobile connectivity is unreliable.

### Simplicity

The application should be easy to use
by schools, parents, and drivers.

### Accessibility

Support practical mobile-first workflows
and reasonable network conditions.

### Scalability

Design for expansion without introducing
unnecessary early complexity.

## 10. Business Success Metrics

Potential metrics:

- Number of onboarded schools
- Number of active students
- Number of completed trips
- Percentage of successful boarding confirmations
- Parent engagement
- GPS update reliability
- Monthly recurring revenue
- School retention
- Notification delivery success

Metric definitions must be finalized
before production reporting.

## 11. Product Risks

Important risks include:

- Unreliable mobile internet
- GPS accuracy limitations
- Battery consumption
- Driver device availability
- Student data privacy
- Unauthorized location access
- School onboarding complexity
- Payment collection
- Operational support
- Regulatory compliance

## 12. Long-Term Vision

iShuliBus should become a trusted
school transportation technology platform
for East Africa.

Future capabilities may include:

- QR-code student identification
- Advanced route optimization
- Transportation analytics
- Automated school billing
- Multi-country support
- Advanced fleet monitoring
- Predictive arrival estimates

These features are not automatically
part of the initial MVP.
